Tier 3 · Association Layer · Phase 2
middle-market.org
The independent professional association for the lower middle market
Strategy

The .org TLD is the single most powerful trust signal in the portfolio. It implies institution, independence, member governance, and professional standards — everything ACG has built over 70 years, repositioned for the digital-first, lower middle market era. This domain is positioned as the "Middle Market Association" (MMA) — an independent professional association with membership tiers, a certification program, chapter creation tools, and a standards-setting role for the lower MM community. It is the most strategically aggressive domain in the entire portfolio.

Importantly, this is distinct from .network: .network is the always-on digital community (LinkedIn for lower MM); .org is the formal association layer with credentials, standards, chapters, and institutional authority. They serve the same audience in complementary ways, and members of one are natural prospects for the other.

Target Audience
PrimaryIndependent sponsors, search fund operators, and lower MM professionals who want an independent credential not tied to ACG membership
Chapter leadsRegional deal professionals who want to create a local chapter under the MMA umbrella — the equivalent of an ACG chapter, but accessible and affordable
InstitutionalCorporate sponsors and advisory firms that want to associate with an independent professional body — without the ACG membership tax
Core Messaging
Association Name
"The Middle Market Association — the independent professional body for the lower middle market deal community."
Differentiation from ACG
Digital-first. Accessible pricing. Lower middle market specific. Independent of a specific association agenda. Focused on the IS, search fund, and lower MM PE professional that ACG underserves.
Certification Pitch
"Certified Middle Market Professional (CMMP) — the independent credential for lower middle market deal practitioners."

Tier 3 · Events · Phase 2
middlemarket.events
The primary events brand for the middle market community — summits, roundtables, and sector conferences
Strategy

The .events TLD is a materially stronger events brand than .online. It gets elevated to Tier 3 Phase 2 as the primary events destination, while .online becomes the digital access companion. The strategic template is ACG's DealMAX — the lower middle market's premier deal conference — but positioned at a lower price point, with tighter lower MM focus, accessible to the independent sponsor and search fund operator who can't justify a $1,500+ ACG membership.

The flagship annual event: Middle Market DealSummit. Quarterly virtual roundtables by sector (healthcare, industrials, business services). Regional in-person dinners under the same brand umbrella. As the portfolio grows, this becomes both an audience acquisition tool (events grow the .media list) and a significant revenue line in its own right.
