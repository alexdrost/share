01 — the business case

Why build this, in one page

Hall CPA's people decisions — hiring, promotions, retention, comp — currently rely on instinct, scattered spreadsheets, and whoever happens to remember. This system replaces that with a single source of truth and surfaces insights that directly drive firm performance.

The shift
Stop reacting to attrition. Start predicting it. Stop guessing at hiring quality. Start measuring it.
This isn't about having an HR database. It's about having the infrastructure to make the right call on people, consistently, before problems become crises — and to prove to partners and clients that Hall runs on data, not vibes.
01 / SEE

Visibility into what's happening

Every talent metric — headcount, attrition, eNPS, performance, comp — visible in real time. No more waiting for quarterly reports to discover a problem that started six months ago.

02 / PREDICT

Prediction of what's next

Flight risk scores update weekly. Cohort attrition patterns surface early warnings. Bench strength analyses show exactly where you're exposed if someone leaves tomorrow.

03 / ACT

Action on what matters most

When the right signal hits the right person at the right time, interventions become surgical. Retention conversations before resignation letters. Promotion discussions before offers elsewhere.

02 — the diagnosis

The problems we're already facing

This isn't a hypothetical system for a hypothetical problem. These are the exact issues surfaced in our recent internal conversations — and here's how Airtable addresses each one, along with the specific data points we'll track to know it's working.

Every issue below is drawn directly from recent leadership discussions about retention, hiring, performance, and onboarding. The pattern is consistent: we have the right instincts, but no infrastructure to act on them consistently. The six problem / solution pairs that follow translate each into a concrete Airtable build with measurable outcomes.

The underlying theme
“When the top two leaders don't have consistent 1:1s, things break down fast.”
— Internal leadership review

6 items · persistent
Recruiting

Reactive hiring, not proactive recruiting

The pain

Over-reliance on Indeed yields lower-caliber candidates. We need to “always be interviewing” A-players — especially after April 15 when we plan to upgrade four key roles — but no one owns a pipeline and no system holds passive candidates warm.

The Airtable fix

Talent Base → Candidates table with Source Type (Inbound Application / Re-Harvest / Proactive Target / Referral / Agency), Track (Tax / Advisory / Operations / Leadership / Admin), and Current Status (Cold Pipeline / Warm Pipeline / In Conversation / Active Process). Days Since Contact formula surfaces stale warm candidates at 90+ days via the Passive Talent Pool interface page. Employee Link FK back to Core HR closes the loop — enables source-to-performance attribution at 12 months.

Data we'll track
  • Pipeline size by Track (Passive Pool page)
  • Source of Hire (Candidates + Sources)
  • Cost per Hire (Sources.Cost ÷ hires)
  • Hiring Quality Score at 90d / 6mo / 1yr by Source Type
  • Days Since Contact ≥ 90 for Warm Pipeline
  • Proactive Target conversion rate
Retention

Critical-person concentration risk

The pain

Spencer is “paramount” — if he leaves, significant disruption. Other key people are equally exposed. There's no visible succession plan, no early warning on comp / equity / stability concerns, and transition plans are built in reaction, not prevention.

The Airtable fix

Succession tables (Phase 4 placeholder, Phase 5 interface): Critical Roles with Criticality Reason, Flight Risk Score lookup, and Ready Now Count rollup. Succession Candidates links employees to roles with Readiness = Ready Now / 12–18 Months / Development Track. Automation: “Critical role with zero ready-now successors → quarterly alert” (Phase 4) forces the conversation before a departure makes it urgent. Surfaced via the Bench Gaps interface page.

Data we'll track
  • Ready Now Count = 0 (alert state)
  • Bench Depth rollup per Critical Role
  • Flight Risk Score of current holder
  • Comp-vs-Band position for critical roles
  • Last Reviewed date (succession staleness)
  • Critical role tenure distribution
Leadership

Leadership gaps invisible until they erupt

The pain

Kaylin is viewed as inexperienced. Jen drives results but her team complains of workload and tone. Today, we rely on partner opinion to spot manager issues — objective signals arrive late, if at all. Misalignment at the top cascades into role-clarity problems below.

The Airtable fix

Reporting Base → Manager Health table with one record per manager, refreshed weekly. Composite Score formula combines Team eNPS Avg, Team Attrition, 1:1 Compliance, and Goal Attainment. Automation: “Manager Health refresh → weekly” (Phase 2) recalculates every Monday after Flight Risk. Surfaced via the Manager Effectiveness Scorecard page in the Executive Leadership View — restricted to CEO + HR Lead.

Data we'll track
  • Composite Score per manager
  • Team eNPS Avg (from Survey Responses)
  • Team Attrition trailing 12 months
  • 1:1 Compliance % (30 days)
  • Goal Attainment avg Completion %
  • Team Size direct reports count
Retention

Retention that's reactive, not proactive

The pain

People resign before anyone noticed the warning signs. The firm has had success with stay interviews in the past — but the practice isn't systematized and isn't applied to the right people at the right time. A “manager retention report” is in the works but lacks a consistent home.

The Airtable fix

Engagement Base → Surveys table with Type = Stay Interview. Automation: “Stay interview overdue → quarterly HR reminder” (Phase 3) triggers for employees with Tenure between 6–36 months and no completed stay interview in last 12 months, sorted by Flight Risk Score — highest risk surfaces first. Flight Risk composite: 30% Pulse signal + 25% Rating signal + 20% Tenure signal + 25% Comp signal. Surfaced via the Stay Interview Tracker interface page.

Data we'll track
  • Flight Risk Score weekly trend
  • Stay Interview coverage %
  • Regrettable Departure rate
  • Pulse Check-ins dimension scores
  • Exit Interview Primary Reason distribution
  • Eligible for Rehire pool count
Onboarding

Ramp-up gap: 4 weeks promised, 3 months real

The pain

Capacity planning assumes four-week ramp. Reality is closer to three months. No 30-day assessment to catch early signals. New hires feel welcomed but drift without structured milestones — and managers don't know whether a new hire is on track until something breaks.

The Airtable fix

Automation (Future Phase): “New employee 30 / 60 / 90 day check-in” triggers form sends at each milestone with notification to manager. Ramp data lives in Core HR → Employment Events (Event Type = Hire, Promotion, etc.) linked to Engagement → Survey Responses with a milestone Survey Type. The “Employee anniversary milestone” automation at 1 / 3 / 5 years (Phase 4) creates a parallel recognition trigger. Actual ramp-to-productivity exposed by linking back to first Performance Review at 90 days.

Data we'll track
  • % meeting 30 / 60 / 90-day milestones
  • Actual time-to-productivity by role
  • 90-day voluntary attrition
  • Hiring-manager satisfaction score
  • Ramp-up curve by department
  • % of new hires with completed check-ins
Hiring Quality

Culture fit, without the technical depth

The pain

New hires consistently fit the culture but lack the technical chops. That shows up later as under-performance, rework, and senior-time drain. Today there's no feedback loop from “interview score” to “actual performance at 12 months.” Our hiring rubric isn't learning.

The Airtable fix

Talent → Interviews table captures Score (1–5), Type (Phone / Video / Technical / Behavioral / Panel / Partner / Final), and Recommendation (Strong Yes / Yes / Neutral / No / Strong No). Once hired, the Candidates.Employee Link FK joins to Reporting → Review Cycle Rollup.Calibrated Rating Score. The Hiring Quality Score metric (Doc 3) averages Calibrated Rating at 90d / 6mo / 1yr grouped by Interviewer — making interviewer calibration visible over time.

Data we'll track
  • Technical at hire vs rating at 12mo
  • Hiring quality score by source
  • Interviewer calibration accuracy
  • % passing 90-day technical milestone
  • Re-work rate in first 180 days
  • Hiring manager post-hire NPS

Real-world context

The seven phases add up to roughly 22 weeks of focused build time (about 5 months). With tax season, client deadlines, leadership bandwidth, and the mid-course adjustments every real rollout requires, a more honest calendar is 8 to 12 months end-to-end. The good news: Phase 1 delivers Core HR plus the full Talent Pipeline & Recruiting Interface — the highest-priority operational workflow — in 5–6 weeks. You get the always-be-recruiting discipline the strategy doc calls for before the rest of the system catches up.
