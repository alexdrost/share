The Origin

It started with one plant on a whim.

James picked up a scorpion pepper plant while buying spring flowers. Unlike the flowers, it actually produced something useful — a massive harvest with nowhere to go but the kitchen.

After weeks of experimenting, the first sauce was born: Scorpion and the Frog. Friends loved it. Samples got passed around. People started asking where they could buy it.

What started as a backyard accident became a growing local brand — built on a simple belief: hot sauce should make your food taste better, not just burn you.

"Built for Flavor, Not Fear."

Many of the superhot peppers in the lineup are still grown directly in James' backyard. Every batch is made in small quantities — because quality doesn't scale the way shortcuts do.

A Backyard Hot Sauce Story
The Discovery Issue

The best hot sauce in town isn't sold in any store.

How a single pepper plant, bought on a whim beside the spring flowers, turned a Berkley backyard into a small-batch obsession.

Made & bottled by James · Berkley, MI

James picked up a scorpion pepper plant while buying spring flowers. Unlike the flowers, it produced something useful — a massive harvest with nowhere to go but the kitchen.

After weeks of experimenting and modifying recipes to fit his own palate, the first sauce was born: Scorpion and the Frog. Friends and family loved it. Samples got passed around. People started asking where they could buy it.

What started as a backyard accident became a growing local brand — built on a simple belief: hot sauce should make your food taste better, not just burn you. Many of the superhot peppers in the lineup are still grown directly in James' backyard, and every batch is made in small quantities.

"Built for flavor, not fear."

origin

EXTRA-FAQFAQ accordion — answers the obvious questions (where to buy, shipping, heat). Pre-empts DMs. Native expand/collapse.
Where can I buy your sauce?

At local Metro Detroit farmers markets, at Dog and Pony Show Brewing in Oak Park, or directly from James — DM on Instagram to ask about current stock. There's no online checkout yet.

Do you ship?

Not yet. The brand is pre–food-certification, so for now everything is local pickup and market sales. Join the mailing list to hear when that changes.

How hot is the hottest one?

Scorpion and the Frog sits at 5 of 5 — built on scorpion peppers. But every sauce is made flavor-first, so even the hot ones are meant to taste like something, not just burn.

Are the peppers really backyard-grown?

Many of the superhots are, yes — grown by James in his Berkley backyard. Every batch is made in small quantities.
