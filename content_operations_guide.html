Production reality: the 80/20 rule for this format

Spend 80% of your intelligence time finding 5 genuinely interesting deals and 20% writing the lead. The lead should never exceed 120 words — if it takes more, the insight isn't sharp enough yet. Write the four rotating sector blurbs in one monthly batch. The community note takes 3 minutes. The consistency of the structure is the feature, not a limitation: readers know what to expect and open the email because of it.

"The first 30 days I'm just listening" — Ryan McGovern on turning around a struggling portfolio company

Ryan McGovern · MD, Alternative Lender · 5× conference attendee7 min read
RM

Ryan McGovern

Managing Director & Investment Committee Member · 20 years in LMM finance

MMW: What's the first thing you do when you walk into a new portfolio company?

I don't touch anything for 30 days. That's the discipline. The instinct is to start fixing things immediately — you've been in diligence four months and have a long list of problems. But the people there know things you don't...

MMW: What's the most common mistake PE firms make in portfolio management?

They confuse urgency with speed. Urgency means knowing which decisions matter most and moving decisively on those. Speed means making all decisions fast — which is a very different thing, and usually a worse one...

The Lower Middle Market Private Equity Landscape: A Complete Guide

The lower middle market — generally defined as companies with $5M–$50M in EBITDA — represents the largest and least-covered segment of the private equity universe. There are more PE firms competing here than anywhere else, more deal types, and more variation in how transactions get structured. This guide explains how the space works.

The contributor pipeline is the force multiplier — prioritize it above everything else

If 40% of monthly long-form content is contributed by practitioners — Operating Partner Spotlights, Deal Anatomy sources, Deal Team Essentials authors — weekly production drops from ~14 hours to ~9 hours at full pace. Building a pipeline of 20 willing contributors through power user outreach is worth more than any tool or automation in the entire stack. Send one contributor request email per week from month 1. After 6 months you have a content bench deep enough to keep the calendar full without burning out a solo operator.
