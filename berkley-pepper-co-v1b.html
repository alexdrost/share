The Origin

It started with a scorpion pepper plant and nowhere to go but up.

While buying spring flowers, James picked up a scorpion pepper plant on a whim. Unlike the flowers, it actually produced something useful — a massive harvest with nowhere to go but the kitchen.

After weeks of experimenting and modifying recipes to fit his own palate, the first sauce was born: Scorpion and the Frog. Friends and family loved it. Samples got passed around. People started asking where they could buy it.

What started as a backyard accident became a growing local brand built on a simple belief: hot sauce should make your food taste better — not just burn you.

"
Built for Flavor, Not Fear.

Many of the superhot peppers in Berkley Pepper Company's lineup are still grown directly in James' backyard. Every batch is made in small quantities — because quality doesn't scale the same way shortcuts do.

Collaboration

When craft beer meets craft heat.

Berkley Pepper Company partnered with Dog and Pony Show Brewing in Oak Park, Michigan to create Smokin' Stout — a sauce built around a real stout beer reduction from the brewery itself.

Reduced stout syrup, smoked ghost peppers, black garlic, chipotles, and roasted vegetables combine into a deeply layered sauce that could only exist as a collaboration. Two local operations, one bottle.
