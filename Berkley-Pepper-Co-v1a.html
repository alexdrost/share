Origin

It started with a pepper plant
and a backyard.

Berkley Pepper Company started almost by accident. While picking up spring flowers, James grabbed a scorpion pepper plant on a whim. Unlike the flowers, the pepper plant actually produced something — a serious harvest that needed a purpose.

After experimenting with hot sauce recipes and bending them to his own taste, he made the first batch: Scorpion and the Frog. Friends and family loved it. Samples started getting passed around. Before long, people were asking where they could buy it.

"What started as a backyard experiment turned into a small-batch operation focused on bold flavor, balanced heat, and real personality."

Many of the superhots are still grown directly in James' backyard in Berkley, Michigan. Each batch is blended, bottled, and labeled by hand — built to be used often, not just talked about.
